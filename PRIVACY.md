# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Wheelson Discord app collects and how it is used. The app includes the Discord bot, the Wheelson Discord Activity, and the web app at <https://tytaniumdev.github.io/MythicPlusDiscordBot/>, all referred to here as "the App." The App is a free, open-source hobby project run by Tyler Holland ("we", "us"). Its full source code is public at <https://github.com/TytaniumDev/MythicPlusDiscordBot>, so you can check everything described here.

## Summary

- The App stores your Discord user ID, display name, the World of Warcraft roles you pick, and, if you link one, your WoW character's name, realm, region, class, and portrait.
- This profile data is **publicly readable**. Anyone who knows your Discord user ID can read it, because other players' clients need it to show you in a lobby.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your data for anything other than running the App.
- Bug reports and feature requests become **public** GitHub issues.

## Information we collect

### From Discord

When the App is in a server, the bot receives information from Discord through its gateway connection:

- **Server information:** server ID, name, icon, and the names and IDs of voice channels.
- **Voice channel membership:** the Discord user ID and display name of each member in a voice channel where a Wheelson lobby is open, or where someone runs `/wheel`.
- **Server member and role information:** your Discord user ID, display name, and server roles. When you use `/wheel`, the bot may read role names such as "Tank" or "Healer" to work out which roles you can play.
- **Commands you run:** the slash command and the text you enter into it, such as a `/bug` description.

The bot does not read or store the content of ordinary chat messages.

### Signing in

By default, the Activity signs you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. If you choose **Sign in with Discord**, Discord asks for your permission to share your account's basic profile (the `identify` scope). We use it only to confirm your Discord user ID, which then becomes your Firebase Authentication account ID so the App knows which player you are. We don't store the Discord access token, and we don't request your email address, server list, or messages.

### Information you give us

- **Player preferences:** your main role, off-spec roles, and utilities (battle res, bloodlust), plus an optional in-game name.
- **Linked WoW character (optional):** the character name, realm, and region you enter. We look up that character through Blizzard's Battle.net API and store its class and portrait image URL.
- **Bug reports, feature requests, and "bad group" reports:** the title and description you write, your Discord display name and user ID, and, for bad group reports, the players and groups from the lobby involved.

### Information generated by using the App

- **Lobby state:** the players in a lobby, who is sitting out, the groups formed, and the round's status.
- **Group history:** the groups formed in your server today, plus a season-long count of how often each pair of players has been grouped together. These are keyed by player name and used to vary the groups.

### Stored on your device

The web app uses your browser's local storage to remember your Discord user ID and servers you've opened recently, so you don't have to pick them again. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. The App does not use tracking cookies or analytics.

### Error reports

When something breaks, error reports are sent to [Sentry](https://sentry.io), our error-monitoring service. Reports from the bot may include your Discord user ID and username, so a problem can be traced to the command that caused it. Reports from the web app don't include your Discord user ID. They may include technical details such as your browser type and the page or action that failed.

## How we use information

We use the information above only to:

- Form Mythic+ groups and show them in Discord and in the Activity.
- Confirm your identity if you use Sign in with Discord.
- Show each player's name, roles, class, and portrait in the lobby.
- Avoid pairing the same players together too often.
- Look up and refresh your linked character's class and portrait.
- File your bug reports and feature requests, and send you a Discord DM when an issue you reported is closed.
- Find and fix bugs, and prevent abuse (for example, rate limiting).

We don't sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

## Where information is stored and who we share it with

We use the following third-party services to run the App. Each one processes data under its own privacy policy.

| Service | What it does | Data involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Discord](https://discord.com/privacy) | Hosts the bot and Activity | Everything received from Discord, and the Sign in with Discord authorization |
| [Google Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) (Firestore, Cloud Functions, Authentication) | Database and backend | Preferences, lobbies, group history, reports, sign-in accounts |
| [Blizzard Battle.net API](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/privacy) | Character lookup | Character name, realm, region |
| [Sentry](https://sentry.io/privacy/) | Error monitoring | Error reports as described above |
| [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) | Hosts the web app and issue tracker | Bug and feature reports (made public) |

**Public data.** Player preferences, lobby documents, and server group history are readable by anyone who knows the relevant Discord user, server, or channel ID. Other players' clients need this to show the lobby. Don't put anything in your in-game name or reports that you want to keep private.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

## How long we keep information

- **Lobbies** are deleted when the last person leaves the voice channel, or automatically 24 hours after their last activity.
- **Today's group history** is replaced each day. **Season pair counts** are replaced when a new Mythic+ season starts.
- **Player preferences** and **sign-in accounts** are kept until you ask us to delete them.
- **Bad group reports** are deleted from our database once they've been turned into a GitHub issue. The GitHub issue stays public.
- **Character lookup cache** entries are refreshed after a day.
- **Error reports** are kept according to Sentry's retention period for our plan.

## Your choices and rights

- **Change your data:** you can update your roles, in-game name, and linked character in the App at any time.
- **Delete your data:** to delete your player preferences and any other data tied to your Discord user ID, contact us (see below). We'll handle requests within 30 days.
- **Stop data collection:** don't use the App's commands or Activity, and leave voice channels where a Wheelson lobby is open. Server admins can remove the bot from their server at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK, or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Contact us to exercise them.

## Children

The App is meant for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirement (13 in most countries, higher in some). We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The latest version is always at this URL, and its history is visible in the repository's commit log. The "Effective date" at the top shows when it last changed.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests:

- Open an issue at <https://github.com/TytaniumDev/MythicPlusDiscordBot/issues>, or
- Use the `/bug` command in any server with the bot.

GitHub issues are public. For a deletion request, you only need to include your Discord user ID. Don't post anything else you want to keep private.
