Flow Summary

  • Screen 1: Pick your Discord name from the voice channel list. Saved to localStorage.
  • Screen 2: Enter WoW name, pick main role, optionally set offspecs/utilities. "I'm Ready" takes you to lobby.
  • Screen 3: Lobby shows all players grouped by role. Your PlayerCard is pinned. Click others to edit via modal. Spin when ready.
  • Returning players: localStorage identity + saved preferences → skip Screen 1 & 2, land directly in lobby as "ready".
