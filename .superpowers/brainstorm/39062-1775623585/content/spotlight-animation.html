Group Card Spotlight Design

What happens between each group reveal

The Sequence

1
Wheels spin & land
Same as today — staggered landing with audio cues. Results appear on each wheel.
2
Wheels fade out, group card slides to center stage
~500ms transition. Wheels area dims/fades. A large, styled group card animates in from below, centered on screen. Victory audio plays.
3
Spotlight holds for 3 seconds
Group card visible center stage with group number heading. Previously revealed group cards stack in the side panel / below.
4
Spotlight card shrinks into side panel, wheels fade back in
~500ms transition. The spotlight card animates down into the accumulated group cards area. Fresh wheels appear with updated candidate pools. Next spin begins automatically.
5
Final group → auto-transition to Results View
After the last group's spotlight, instead of showing wheels again, fade to the full Results View with all groups displayed.
